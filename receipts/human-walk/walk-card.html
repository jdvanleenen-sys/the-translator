4. The currency — the honest part

The form says not in source. Look at the receipt: is there a $, USD, or any currency next to the total? No. A dishonest tool would guess “$.” This one refused. That’s the whole point.

6. Nothing was hidden

Why this matters

Every line on the form is either pointing at a receipt line you can see with your own eyes, or it honestly says “not in source.” Nothing was guessed, computed, or made up.
